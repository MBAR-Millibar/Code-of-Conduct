# Millibar.io Code of Conduct

> Translations: [العربية](translations/ar.md) · [Български](translations/bg.md) · [Čeština](translations/cs.md) · [Deutsch](translations/de.md) · [Español](translations/es.md) · [Français](translations/fr.md) · [Italiano](translations/it.md) · [日本語](translations/ja.md) · [Polski](translations/pl.md) · [Русский](translations/ru.md)

## Our pledge

We, the people who build, use and contribute to Millibar.io, pledge to make
taking part in our community welcoming and safe for everyone. That includes our
team, contributors, users and partners. It applies regardless of age, body,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
appearance, religion (or lack of religion), or sexual orientation.

## Scope

This code applies in every Millibar space: our apps and platforms, community
channels and social media, GitHub repositories, email, meetings, events and work
travel, and in one-on-one conversations about Millibar matters. It also applies
whenever someone represents Millibar.io, for example through an official account
or email, or at a talk or booth. It applies to everyone who takes part, paid or
unpaid, including founders, team members, investors, sponsors, partners and
guests. When an event venue is shared with the public, we treat everyone there
with the same respect.

## What we encourage

- Be kind, honest and respectful, including when we disagree.
- Try collaboration before conflict.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise, and repair the harm.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.
- Respect privacy and personal data.
- Look out for each other and speak up when something is wrong.
- If you notice a dangerous situation, someone in distress, or a breach of this
  code, tell us, even if it seems minor.

Communities mirror the societies around them, including their inequalities and
abuses of power. We take active steps to counter them. If you see someone making
an extra effort to make our community welcoming, tell us: we want to know.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, intimidation, bullying, stalking or following, online or offline.
- Discrimination, or demeaning comments about someone's identity or background.
- Sexualised language or imagery (including in slides and presentations),
  unwanted sexual attention, or unwelcome physical contact. "No", or the
  absence of a clear "yes", means no.
- Inappropriate depictions of violence, including in slides and presentations.
- Personal attacks and insults.
- Photographing or recording someone against their wishes.
- Sharing anyone's private information without permission, including data from
  our platforms.
- Impersonation, fake profiles, or false claims about identity or credentials.
- Spam and unsolicited promotion.
- Threats, or anything that puts someone's safety at risk.
- Repeatedly disrupting conversations, meetings or events.

## Power and influence

We never use a position of influence to seek personal, romantic or sexual
favours. That covers influence over hiring, partnerships, investment, access,
visibility or future work. We do not make romantic or sexual advances toward
people over whom we hold influence in a Millibar context. Any exchange
arrangement (e.g. work for services, content for access) is agreed in writing
and never depends on anything personal.

## Events and travel

No pressure around alcohol, accommodation or after-hours socialising. Anyone can
leave a situation that feels unsafe without any consequences for their work. If
you ask, we will help you get somewhere safe or contact local authorities. At
in-person events, someone from the team will stay with you or accompany you if
you wish.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that breaks this code, or have any other
concern, tell us as soon as you can. You do not need to be sure that something
breaks the code: we would rather hear about it. Contact
**[conduct@millibar.io](mailto:conduct@millibar.io)** or any team member you
trust.

- We acknowledge reports within 48 hours.
- We handle reports confidentially and fairly, and we listen first.
- There is no retaliation for reporting in good faith.
- We will tell you what we can about the outcome.

## Addressing and repairing harm

Our aim is to teach, not to punish. We all make mistakes, and most problems come
from misunderstanding or not knowing better. Most can be solved with a
conversation. We want people to understand the impact of what they did, repair
the harm, and keep taking part.

If someone tells you that your behaviour hurt them or made them uncomfortable,
stop, listen and take it seriously, even if you did not mean it that way.

We usually go step by step, starting with the lightest response that works:

1. **Conversation:** a private conversation to understand what happened, explain
   the impact, and agree how to put it right (e.g. an apology).
2. **Warning:** a private, written reminder of the code, with clear expectations
   for the future.
3. **Limited participation:** a time-limited restriction on certain spaces or
   interactions, with support to return.
4. **Temporary break:** a pause from all Millibar spaces for a set period,
   with clear conditions for coming back.
5. **Permanent removal:** only as a last resort, when harm continues or someone's
   safety is at risk.

When someone's safety is at risk, we may need to act straight away. For team
members, contractors and partners, serious or repeated breaches can also lead to
ending the collaboration or contract. Where appropriate, and with the wishes of
the person affected in mind, we involve the authorities.

## Fair process

If you believe you were reported unfairly or treated unfairly in a decision,
write to [conduct@millibar.io](mailto:conduct@millibar.io) with a short description of your
concern. Both sides are heard before a final decision, and everyone involved is
treated with respect.

## Review

We review this code at least once a year and after any report that shows it
needs to change.
